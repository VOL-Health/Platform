Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

VOL Health is designed to support operational visibility and leadership decision support for senior living communities. We believe operational information should be handled thoughtfully, responsibly, and with respect for organizational sensitivity.

Information Shared With VOL

Information shared with VOL Health may include contact information, operational discussion details, pilot collaboration materials, uploaded operational datasets, and other information voluntarily provided through product evaluations or community engagement.

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to support operational analysis, product improvement, pilot collaboration, leadership visibility insights, and communication related to VOL Health services and platform development.

Data Handling Philosophy

VOL Health is designed to support operational understanding while minimizing unnecessary data collection. We aim to handle operational information responsibly, maintain limited access where appropriate, and respect the sensitivity of community operational environments.

External Sharing

VOL Health does not sell operational or organizational data provided through pilot discussions, evaluations, or platform collaboration activities.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or operational data handling practices may be directed through the Contact Us page.
