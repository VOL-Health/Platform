Current Release + Roadmap

SHAI is the evolving operational alignment layer of VOL Health. Today, it helps translate operating signals into prioritized stabilization guidance, executive recommendations, and clearer next actions. VOL rEDi is the lightweight operational readiness layer that demonstrates SHAI's practical direction: turning synthesized operating context into clear next actions for senior living leadership teams. Over time, SHAI is being developed to support deeper workflow coordination, external operating data sources, strategic partner integrations, and workforce response planning.
